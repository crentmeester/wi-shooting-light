# Wisconsin Shooting Light

Wisconsin legal shooting hours for your zone, from the official DNR 2026–27 shooting-hours tables, plus which seasons are open for the licenses you hold.

**Open it:** https://crentmeester.github.io/wi-shooting-light/

- Tap **Use my location** to pick your shooting-hours zone by GPS, or tap the map.
- Tick your licenses to see what you can hunt today.
- Add it to your home screen (Chrome menu → Add to Home screen). It works offline after the first visit.

Your licenses and zone are saved only on your own phone.

Always confirm against the official [Wisconsin hunting regulations](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/hunt/regulations). If anything here differs from the DNR's printed tables, the DNR is right.
