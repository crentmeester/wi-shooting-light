Shooting hours come straight from the DNR's official 2026–27 Northern and Southern Zone A tables (Sept. 1, 2026 – May 31, 2027), plus your zone's added minutes, in Central Time. Dates outside those tables (June 1 onward) are calculated and marked. Season dates are from the DNR's Fall 2026–Spring 2027 regulations. Unit-specific rules, CWD/antlerless quotas, and county closures still apply. Always check the official Wisconsin hunting regulations or the Go Wild app before you go.
